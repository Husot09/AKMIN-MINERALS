Materials

Feldspar and quartz, understood in depth.

Feldspar and quartz are used mainly in the ceramic and glass industries, but they also have a wide range of applications in other sectors. Drawing on the technical knowledge we have built from producing and processing these minerals, Akmin develops raw material solutions tailored to our customers' needs. We also research new industrial uses for the by-products and waste materials generated during production.

Feldspar

Feldspar is a core flux for both the ceramic and glass industries. Its alkali and alkaline-earth oxides drive vitrification in ceramic bodies and glazes, and serve as a flux and alumina source in glass batches.

  • Primary flux for ceramic bodies and glazes
  • Contributes Na₂O, K₂O and CaO oxides plus alumina (Al₂O₃) to the melt
  • Sensitive to chemistry consistency and particle size
  • Sourced from Çine, the world's most important feldspar region
Typical applications
Ceramic bodies Tiles Sanitaryware Glazes Glass

Quartz

Quartz provides the silica backbone of ceramic, glass and industrial formulations. Purity, sizing and thermal behavior determine its value in the final product.

  • High-purity silica for ceramic, glass and industrial use
  • Controls thermal expansion and structural stability
  • Particle size distribution tailored to the application
  • Consistent quality for demanding formulations
Typical applications
Ceramic bodies Glazes Glass Industrial
