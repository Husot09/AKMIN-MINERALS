About us

Rooted in one of the world's leading feldspar regions.

Akmin combines decades of experience in mineral development with technical expertise across feldspar exploration, processing and supply.

Our story

Four decades in Turkish feldspar.

Akmin's roots go back to the early 1980s, alongside the development of Turkey's feldspar industry. Built on deep operational experience in flotation, beneficiation, production and export, the company grew into Turkey's largest feldspar exporter in 2001–2002.

Today, Akmin applies this experience across the feldspar and quartz value chain: combining technical research, enrichment know-how, ceramic formulation support and applied R&D to develop new applications for industrial minerals.

Milestones

From pioneers to mineral developers.

  • Early 1980s

    Pioneering roots

    The team's feldspar experience dates back to the early 1980s in Çine, Aydın, one of Turkey's leading feldspar regions.

  • First company

    Acquired by Sibelco

    The team's first operation was acquired by Sibelco Group.

  • Second company

    Sold to a listed Turkish company

    The second company was later sold to a Turkish company listed on the Istanbul Stock Exchange.

  • Today

    Long-term resource development

    Akmin is now focused on its latest feldspar and quartz project: a large-scale deposit developed over more than ten years of geological, technical and enrichment work. The project aims to turn lower-grade feldspar resources into economically viable reserves, anticipating the long-term decline of high-quality feldspar deposits worldwide.

How we work

Akmin develops mineral solutions that are technically reliable, economically viable and environmentally responsible. Our priorities are consistent raw material quality, strong technical expertise and creating long-term value for our customers.
